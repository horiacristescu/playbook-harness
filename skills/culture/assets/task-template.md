## Intent
Select, with evidence, what from the project's lived history is worth
transmitting to future participants, why it is worth carrying, and in which
future contexts it is likely to reduce rediscovery cost, prevent characteristic
mistakes, or preserve valuable possibilities.

## Why
A project cannot carry its entire past into every future context. This task
treats the project lifespan like a larger-scale attention problem: identify
reusable context, compress it without erasing the reasons that make it
adaptable, and route it to later situations where its value exceeds its loading
cost.

## Selection and interpretation

- [ ] Deep-read each pivotal candidate in its original context and trace its lineage forward. Prefer causal forms such as `problem → correction → changed practice → later reuse` or `observation → objection → narrower claim → new application` over quotation collections.
- [ ] For every surviving candidate, judge context worth without a rigid score: what future situation would retrieve it, what rediscovery or recurrence cost does it avoid, what possibility does it preserve, how often or deeply was it reused, and what is the smallest form that retains its useful reason?
- [ ] Run an independent omission pass that does not follow the draft headings. Search for correction language, repeated rediscovery, abandoned plans, failures, changes of mind, unnamed behavioral changes, relational boundaries, and unresolved tensions; update the ledger with evidence.
- [ ] Run a supersession and authorship pass. Record important `earlier formulation → pressure/counterexample → current formulation` chains, separate user/team assertions from agent inference, and retain uncertainty where evidence does not close it.
- [ ] Critique the ledger: demote institutional mythology, flattering origin stories, boilerplate frequency, isolated eloquence, and material already cheaper to retrieve from a better source. Record exclusions and why they should not be transmitted.

## Convergence and transmission

- [ ] Convergence pass 1: traverse the primary sources against the draft using a different route from the original synthesis. Record new cultural kinds and material changes to preservation reasons; revise the artifact and ledger.
- [ ] Convergence pass 2: repeat with an omission/supersession lens. If this or the prior pass changed the inheritance's shape, add another unchecked convergence gate immediately; stop only after two consecutive stable passes.
- [ ] Verify every citation exists, every entry explains its future worth, revised positions are not timeless doctrine, mundane recurring lessons were not displaced by eloquence, authorship and privacy are correct, and unresolved questions remain visibly unresolved.

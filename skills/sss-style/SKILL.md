# SSS Style

Synthetic, short, simple.

**Synthetic**: capture the essence, not the coverage. Say what changes the
reader's model or action; drop the rest. Each thing said once.

**Short**: the fewest sentences that hold the content. If a section can be a
sentence, make it a sentence. No preamble, no wrap-up.

**Simple**: plain words, concrete nouns, active verbs. One idea per sentence.
A reader in a hurry gets everything on the first pass.

## Test

Delete a sentence. Did the reader lose anything they needed? If not, it stays
deleted. Apply until nothing more comes out.

## Not

Not telegraphic: keep articles and grammar, cut content-free content, not
words at random. Not vague: compression that loses the file name, the number,
or the failure case went too far. When essence and brevity conflict, essence
wins.
